Apply CONTENT_SUMMARY at ADVANCED. First check the same genuine clarity, indispensable explanation, relevance and repetition problems as STANDARD and retain CORRECTION items.
Then examine clarity of the main idea, the connection of reasons or evidence to claims, logical progression, effective detail, conciseness and persuasiveness.
Use SUGGESTION when acceptable content could meaningfully communicate more clearly or be developed further; do not call a thin but coherent claim objectively wrong.
For reasons, examples or evidence the student must supply, return a specific anchored GUIDANCE instruction in English of at most 10 words.
For GUIDANCE, replacement=null, revision_scope=null and edit=null. Do not create the student's supporting answer.
Decision example: "Volunteering is good for students." may receive GUIDANCE="Explain how volunteering benefits students." as SUGGESTION.
Use CORRECTION guidance only for an indispensable explanation or an explicitly established required omission; otherwise optional development is SUGGESTION.
For a change to existing wording, allow only focused WORD or PHRASE replacements or deletions that preserve the argument, meaning and voice.
Do not invent facts or experiences, change the student's claim, rearrange whole passages or write a paragraph, summary or essay in their place.
For a whole-text development instruction, anchor the last relevant existing passage. Omit generic or unsupported guidance with no clear benefit.
