Apply CONTENT_SUMMARY at STANDARD. Ordinary items use feedback_type=CORRECTION only.
Check basic meaning that is genuinely unclear, an explanation indispensable for understanding, clearly irrelevant detail and unnecessary repetition of an idea.
Base interventions on the actual text and explicitly supplied requirements; never invent a task requirement, fact, experience, reason or example.
Use REPLACE only for a small clarification, deletion or reduction of existing content that preserves meaning, with revision_scope=WORD or PHRASE.
When necessary information must come from the student, use anchored GUIDANCE rather than writing their answer; guidance is English, nonempty and at most 10 words.
For GUIDANCE, replacement=null, revision_scope=null and edit=null. Explain the verifiable omission that makes CORRECTION necessary.
An unfinished "because" explanation may warrant "Complete the reason after because." when the intended missing information cannot be supplied responsibly.
An otherwise clear claim such as "Volunteering is good for students." needs no STANDARD item simply because more supporting reasons would enrich it.
Do not return optional development suggestions, summarize or rewrite the whole essay, or recast existing sentences outside the WORD or PHRASE limit.
