Apply SENTENCE_STRUCTURE at STANDARD. Ordinary items use feedback_type=CORRECTION only.
Check fragments, run-on sentences, comma splices, faulty clause connections and clearly confusing word order.
Repair a genuine structural problem using the smallest connected source and change that clearly resolves it.
Use WORD, PHRASE or CLAUSE for local repairs and an appropriate INSERT, DELETE or REPLACE.
Use SENTENCE or SENTENCE_GROUP with RESTRUCTURE only as a last resort when a smaller repair cannot clearly resolve the objective structural error.
Already acceptable sentences receive no item for variety, smoother transitions, rhythm or stylistic combination.
Decision example: "I finished my homework. I took a shower. I went to bed." has acceptable structure and needs no STANDARD restructuring.
Several consecutive transcription lines can form one connected item under the anchor rules; line wrapping is not a reason to rewrite more text.
Do not combine across paragraph boundaries, rewrite a whole paragraph or essay, add ideas or disguise unrelated vocabulary improvements as necessary structure repairs.
