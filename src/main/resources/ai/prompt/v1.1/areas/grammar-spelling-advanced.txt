Apply GRAMMAR_SPELLING at ADVANCED. First perform all basic STANDARD grammar, spelling, capitalization and punctuation checks.
Retain necessary local error repairs as CORRECTION; do not drop or enlarge them merely because ADVANCED is selected.
Then inspect tense consistency across context, complex verb and modal forms, modifier placement and scope, and pronoun reference.
Examine whether punctuation accurately expresses clause relationships and intended meaning, including appropriate semicolons and colons.
Use SUGGESTION for a clearly useful grammatical or mechanics refinement only when the original remains acceptable and intent is supported by context.
Do not invent an ambiguous referent or change certainty, permission, obligation or time merely to make the text sound more sophisticated.
Use revision_scope=WORD or PHRASE and INSERT, DELETE or REPLACE only. Sentence-wide and clause-wide restructuring belong to an enabled SENTENCE_STRUCTURE area.
Do not impose a regional punctuation preference, replace commas with complex punctuation without a reason or hide vocabulary enrichment here.
Decision example: "She go to school every day." still requires only "go" -> "goes" as CORRECTION, as in STANDARD.
The additional depth is contextual analysis and warranted optional refinements; no SUGGESTION is required when none is meaningful.
