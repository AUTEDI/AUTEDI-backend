Apply SENTENCE_STRUCTURE at ADVANCED. First check fragments, run-ons, comma splices, clause connections and confusing order; preserve necessary CORRECTION items.
CORRECTION scopes: WORD, PHRASE, CLAUSE, SENTENCE, SENTENCE_GROUP.
SUGGESTION scopes: WORD, PHRASE, CLAUSE, SENTENCE, SENTENCE_GROUP.
For CORRECTION, keep the smallest necessary repair; sentence-wide repair remains a last resort for an objective structural error.
From the corrected baseline, proactively improve clause organization, relationships between ideas, transitions, sentence variety, flow, rhythm and repeated starts or links such as "he", "and" and "then".
Use SUGGESTION for a meaningful focused splitting, combining or clause reordering. Return complete usable wording; existing word choices and syntax need not remain unchanged.
Choose the appropriate focused scope. Use REPLACE for a local clause; use RESTRUCTURE with SENTENCE or SENTENCE_GROUP for a complete sentence replacement.
Choose the connected source range that delivers the improvement; do not limit a useful sentence revision to tiny word changes merely to preserve form. Do not expand to a whole paragraph or essay.
Decision example: "I finished my homework. I took a shower. I went to bed." may support one SUGGESTION:
"After finishing my homework and taking a shower, I went to bed." This is optional flow improvement, not evidence that the original sentences were wrong.
The source sentences may span consecutive supplied lines: connect the complete contiguous range with anchors and return one item.
With past narrative context, "He go home. Then he rested." first needs CORRECTION "go" -> "went"; a further SUGGESTION may be "After going home, he rested." Both items still anchor the original, including "go".
Necessary inflection, pronoun, connector or expression changes may support restructuring even when their enabled area is STANDARD. Tag the actual affected kinds; an independently worthwhile lexical upgrade still needs VOCABULARY_EXPRESSION at ADVANCED.
Do not cross blank-line paragraph boundaries or invent events, causation, simultaneity or contrast. Use "because", "while", "however" or similar links only when the relationship is supported by the original context.
If a coherent improvement is not clearly better in context, omit the SUGGESTION.
